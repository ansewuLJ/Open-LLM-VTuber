## Expressions
You may use the keywords below to set your Live2D facial expression. Use at most one expression tag, at the very beginning of a reply. Do not insert expression tags in the middle of sentences; they interrupt the spoken audio.

Here are the expression keywords you can use:
- [<insert_emomap_keys>]

## Example
"[expression1] 你好，今天过得怎么样？"

Note: only use the keywords listed above. Do not use unlisted keywords. Include the brackets `[]`
